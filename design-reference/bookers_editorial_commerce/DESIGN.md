## Brand & Style

This design system blends contemporary editorial gravitas with the welcoming vitality of an upscale modern Indonesian book café and independent publisher. The aesthetic merges tactile literary qualities (deckle-edge cream stocks, deliberate serif headlines, considered line lengths) with sharp, intuitive digital commerce principles.

### Visual Tone & Philosophy
- **Modern Literary Warmth:** A distinct rejection of cold, transactional tech interfaces. Surfaces evoke natural unbleached book paper, balanced by rich forest greens and an energizing, sun-warmed Indonesian citrus orange.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Text layout prioritizes effortless long-form reading, rhythmic pacing, and clear typographic hierarchy reminiscent of prestige publication covers and literary journals.
- **Tactile Accessibility:** High-contrast text, generous touch points, tactile interactive feedback, and soft ambient shadows create an interface that feels substantial, curated, and approachable.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-energy warmth with deep, organic botanical tones and textured paper neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Tangerine / Sun-Warmed Terracotta):** `#F97316` acts as the primary action color, driving checkout flows, badges, call-to-actions, and interactive accents. Its deeper counterpart `#EA580C` provides necessary hover depth and accessible text-on-light-background contrast.
- **Secondary & Accent (Emerald & Deep Forest):** `#059669` and `#047857` introduce institutional stability, author quotes, literary curation badges, and success confirmations. The soft mint tint `#ECFDF5` provides a restful background for tags, badges, and notification panels.
- **Neutrals & Foundation:**
  - `Surface Base (Warm Cream Paper):` `#FDFBF7` provides warm, tactile body backgrounds that reduce eye fatigue compared to pure harsh white.
  - `Surface Raised (Pure Paper):` `#FFFFFF` elevates cards, modals, and content containers.
  - `Text & Contrast:** Slate darks `#0F172A` (Headlines, Body Primary) and `#334155` (Secondary metadata, authors, publishing dates) maintain rigorous WCAG AAA compliance against both `#FFFFFF` and `#FDFBF7`.
  - `Structural Borders:** `#E2E8F0` keeps card boundaries defined without creating harsh gridlines.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs **Merriweather** for literary narrative warmth with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for crisp, humanistic UI mechanics and metadata clarity.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Guidelines
- **Headlines & Titles:** Merriweather is reserved for editorial highlights, book titles, major section headers, and pull quotes. Line heights remain generous to celebrate classical book setting conventions.
- **User Interface & Commerce:** Plus Jakarta Sans is applied to navigation, pricing, metadata (ISBN, publisher details, author tags), filter controls, and transactional inputs.
- **Reading Pacing:** Long-form synopses and author interviews should never exceed 68 characters per line (`max-w-prose`) to preserve optimal legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an airy, breathable 12-column grid system on desktop (max width `1320px`), reflowing to an 8-column grid on tablet and a single-to-two-column structure on mobile devices.

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Canvas Margins:** Generous page margins frame content like the margins of an art book, creating physical calmness rather than cramped digital density.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section breaks employ expanded breathing room (`space-xl` multiplied for thematic breaks) to mimic curated bookstore displays rather than endless supermarket shelves.
- **Book Display Grids:** Product catalog grids prioritize aspect-ratio preservation for cover art (standard 2:3 or 1:1.5 book cover ratios) with consistent vertical gaps between price blocks and titles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp corporate drop shadows in favor of warm, ambient, paper-inspired elevation tiers.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FDFBF7` canvas baseline. No shadow. Used for page backgrounds and content dividers.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface lifted with an ambient warm shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Outlined with a hairline border `#E2E8F0` at 1px.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts):** Book cards on hover and dropdown selects elevate dynamically: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.06)`. Note the subtle warm orange tint in the ambient shadow layer.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Quick View):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a muted backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm` over `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design uses rounded forms (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) to soften interfaces, referencing the smooth edges of pressed book blocks, curved bookmarks, and comfortable handheld physical objects.

### Shape Distribution
- **Cards & Shelves:** Rounded with `1rem` (`rounded-xl`) or `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) for featured publisher spotlights and banners.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Interactive touch elements use rounded corners (`0.75rem` to full pill geometry for category chips), providing comfortable tap affordances.
- **Cover Imagery:** Book jackets within cards use a tight `0.5rem` radius with subtle inner spine shadows to replicate tangible paperbacks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#F97316`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold weight. Smooth transition to `#EA580C` on hover with a micro-lift (`translate-y-[-1px]`). Rounded `0.75rem`. Focus ring uses high-contrast emerald `#059669` outline with 2px offset.
- **Secondary:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`. Ideal for "Add to Wishlist" or "Sample Chapter".
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#0F172A`, underline on hover, used for secondary actions like "View All Titles".

### Cards (Book & Publication)
- Built on a crisp `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-xl` borders and `1px` subtle outline (`#E2E8F0`).
- Features a designated 2:3 aspect ratio book cover area, an editorial title set in Merriweather (`title-md`), author metadata in slate `#334155`, and localized price formatting in bold Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Integrated quick-action floating icons (e.g., bookmark icon) in the upper corner with a frosted `#FFFFFF`/90 background.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Curated / Bestseller Badges:** `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text and 1px border `#10B981` (20% opacity).
- **Genre Filters / Category Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.5rem 1rem`. Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` border `#E2E8F0` text `#334155`. Active state: `#0F172A` fill with white text, or `#F97316` fill for promotional campaigns.

### Inputs & Search
- Inputs use `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with 1.5px border `#E2E8F0`, rounded `0.75rem`. Text in `#0F172A` with `#64748B` placeholder styling.
- On focus: Crisp `#F97316` border with a 3px soft tint ring (`rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`).
- Search fields include an integrated category dropdown for filtering by "Judul" (Title), "Penulis" (Author), or "ISBN".

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with `0.375rem` rounding for checkboxes; concentric circles for radios.
- Inactive: Border `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active: `#059669` fill with crisp white checkmark iconography.

### Specialized Bookstore Components
- **Reading Progress Bar:** Slim 4px bar with `#ECFDF5` track and `#059669` fill for preview chapters.
- **Author Pull-Quote Card:** Cream background (`#FDFBF7`), left border 4px in `#F97316`, Merriweather italic text with an author portrait thumbnail.
